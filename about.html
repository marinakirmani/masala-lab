About

The science of flavour

Masala Lab is a spice brand I created for this module. The brief asked us to make a 3D web app showing three branded products. Instead of doing the usual coke bottle example, I decided to make something based around Indian/ Pakistani spices because it felt more personal to me as I love cooking and I am Pakistani. The idea behind Masala Lab is to show these spices in a clean 3d model. The name is also a small nod to Krish Ashok’s book ‘Masala Lab’.

The three jars

The site features three spices: Haldi (turmeric), Lal Mirch (red chilli), and Garam Masala (the house blend). They share the same jar design but each has its own label, information on the label and spice colour. objects.

Design

I chose a magazine style layout. The homepage scrolls and each jar gets its own section and a coloured stripe matching its spice. If you click a jar's name you go to its own page with a 3D viewer.

The colour palette is built around the cream and orange from the labels The header bar on each jar's detail page matches the colour of the spivce.

Typography is a mix: Georgia for headings and sans-serif for the other labels.

3D modelling

Each jar was built in Blender and exported as glTF (.glb). The jar body is three part transparent glass mesh (top rim, body, bottom rim). A separate curved label mesh sits on the front rather than a flat UV projection, so the label keeps its aspect ratio and is readable from any angle. The lid has a 70 frame opening animation that Three.js implements via AnimationMixer.

Web implementation

The 3D viewer uses Three.js loaded as an ES module using an import map. (the approach from the supplementary handout). Each page reads its model filename from a data attribute on the viewer container.

Features available on each detail page:

  • Orbit / zoom
  • Wireframe toggle
  • Lid open / close animation
  • Auto-rotate and a reset-view button
  • Lighting controls a headlight on/off

Deeper understanding

  • The jar body is made of see-through glass so you can view the spice inside
  • The label is a separate curved shape on the front of the jar, not just a flat image wrapped around it
  • The lid opens and closes with an animation when you click the button
  • One JavaScript file handles the 3D viewer on all three spice pages, instead of duplicating code

    • Testing

    I tested the site in Chrome and Safari and on my iPhone. The 3D jars load on each page, you can drag to rotate them on a mouse or with your finger on a phone. the wireframe button works on every part of the jar and the lid opens and closes when you click and the animation. I built the site using VS Code.
